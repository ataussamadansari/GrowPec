## 3. Cross-Phase Integration Analysis

### Navigation Architecture
The panel groups all 12 administrative surfaces into 4 clear navigation domains in `AdminPanelProvider.php`:
1. **College Management**: `CollegeResource` (sort: 1)
2. **Academics & Geography**: `StateResource` (1), `CityResource` (2), `StreamResource` (3), `CourseResource` (4), `SpecializationResource` (5)
3. **Leads & Reviews**: `LeadResource` (1), `CollegeReviewResource` (2)
4. **Marketing & Settings**: `BannerResource` (1), `PartnerResource` (2), `LeadershipMessageResource` (3), `CenterLoginResource` (4), `ManageSettings` (5)

### Relational & Workflow Integrity
- **College Aggregate Hierarchy:** The parent `College` record integrates with 8 child entities through native Filament RelationManagers. Changes to child courses, accreditations, facilities, placement packages, scholarships, and FAQs are immediately reflected on the public college detail page (`/college/{slug}`).
- **Review Moderation Pipeline:** Public guests submit reviews at `/college/{slug}/reviews` (throttled and validated). Reviews land in `pending` status. They immediately surface in:
  1. The dashboard `PendingReviewsWidget` for instant 1-click moderation.
  2. The `CollegeReviewResource` table with dedicated status tabs (`All`, `Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`).
  3. Once approved, `status = 'approved'` and `is_verified = true`, publishing the review to the public website showcase.
- **Cache Invalidation:** Updates made via `CenterLoginResource` and `ManageSettings` immediately invalidate cached instances in `SystemSetting` and `CenterLogin`, ensuring instant public website updates without stale cache issues.
